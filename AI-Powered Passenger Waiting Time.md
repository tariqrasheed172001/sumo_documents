# **Feature Proposal: AI-Powered Passenger Waiting Time Prediction**

## **Problem Statement**
Passengers waiting at sumo stands often face uncertainty regarding how long they will have to wait to secure a seat. This lack of information leads to frustration, inefficiency, and in some cases, passengers leaving to seek alternative transport options.

---

## **Proposed Solution**
Introduce an AI-powered feature that predicts the estimated waiting time for passengers at their current sumo stand. This prediction will be based on historical data, current passenger volumes, and real-time conditions, providing passengers with greater transparency and control over their travel experience.

---

## **Key Benefits**

1. **Increased Passenger Satisfaction:**
   - Provide passengers with a clear estimate of how long they need to wait, reducing uncertainty and frustration.

2. **Improved Stand Efficiency:**
   - Passengers can make informed decisions, preventing overcrowding and enhancing the flow at busy stands.

3. **Enhanced Trust in the Service:**
   - Transparency fosters trust, encouraging passengers to rely more on the sumo stand network.

4. **Reduced Drop-offs:**
   - Fewer passengers will leave the stand out of frustration or uncertainty, ensuring steady business for drivers.

---

## **Core Features**
1. **Waiting Time Estimation:**
   - Display the estimated time a passenger will need to wait before securing a seat.

2. **Dynamic Updates:**
   - Adjust waiting time predictions in real-time based on changing conditions, such as the arrival of additional vehicles or fluctuations in passenger volume.

3. **User Notifications:**
   - Alert passengers if their waiting time changes significantly, allowing them to plan accordingly.

4. **Accessibility:**
   - Display waiting times prominently in the passenger app for quick and easy access.

---

This feature aims to provide passengers with a seamless, predictable travel experience, ensuring they feel informed and valued while waiting for their ride.
