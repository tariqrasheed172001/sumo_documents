# **Feature Proposal: Route Breakdown via Sumo Stands**

## **Problem Solved**
Travelers often struggle to navigate their journey between multiple sumo stands when traveling to unfamiliar destinations. This can lead to confusion, wasted time, and missed rides, especially for passengers unfamiliar with the route.

This feature will simplify the process by providing a clear breakdown of the trip, detailing all intermediate sumo stands required to reach the destination efficiently.

---

## **Feature Description**

### **Core Functionality**
1. **Trip Input**: Passengers enter their starting location and destination (e.g., Bandipora to Pulwama).
2. **Intermediate Sumo Stands**: The app identifies and lists all sumo stands along the route, including:
   - The nearest sumo stand to the user's starting location.
   - Intermediate sumo stands where passengers need to transition to another sumo.
   - The final sumo stand near the destination.
3. **Step-by-Step Journey Plan**: A detailed breakdown of the trip is provided, including:
   - Sumo stand names for each leg of the journey.
   - Estimated time to travel between stands.
   - Fare estimates for each leg.

---

## **How It Works**
1. **Route Calculation**:
   - Passengers search for their destination in the app.
   - The system identifies the optimal route by analyzing connections between sumo stands.

2. **Route Breakdown**:
   - A detailed guide is generated with step-by-step instructions for each sumo stand transition.
   - Passengers can view estimated travel times and fare details for every segment of the journey.

3. **Offline Access**:
   - Users can save the trip breakdown for offline use in areas with poor connectivity.

---

## **Benefits**
1. **Guidance for New Passengers**:
   - Helps travelers unfamiliar with routes navigate seamlessly between sumo stands.

2. **Reduced Confusion**:
   - Eliminates the guesswork in finding the correct sumo stands for each leg of the trip.

3. **Time Efficiency**:
   - Optimizes travel by suggesting the quickest and most direct route.

4. **Improved Travel Confidence**:
   - Passengers feel more confident and secure knowing their journey is clearly planned.

---

## **Future Enhancements**
1. **Fare Aggregation**:
   - Option to calculate and display the total fare for the entire journey upfront.

2. **Driver Collaboration**:
   - Drivers register their availability and routes to enhance real-time accuracy.

3. **Feedback System**:
   - Allow passengers to provide feedback on specific sumo stands or routes to improve service quality.
