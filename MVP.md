# A minimum viable product (MVP)

## Overview
The **Dynamic Sumo Scheduling and Seat Management System** is designed to streamline the scheduling and seat management of passenger vehicles like Sumos. This MVP focuses on delivering essential functionalities to ensure smooth operations, efficient seat utilization, and enhanced communication between passengers and drivers.  

---

## MVP Functionalities  

### 1. **Authentication for Passengers and Drivers**
   - Secure login and registration for both passengers and drivers.
   - Basic profile management to maintain accountability.

### 2. **Real-Time Seat and Location Tracking**
   - Tracks available seats in real-time for passengers and drivers.
   - Integrates with GPS or mapping services for accurate vehicle location.

### 3. **Dynamic Booking**
   - Allows passengers to reserve available seats dynamically.
   - Provides real-time updates on seat availability and booking status.

### 4. **Passenger and Driver Notifications**
   - Sends instant notifications for:
     - New bookings.
     - Departure times.
     - Delays.
     - Seat availability updates.

### 5. **Sumo Stand Queue Management**
   - Virtual queue system for organized seat allocation.
   - Minimizes confusion and reduces waiting times at stands.

### 6. **Communication System**
   - Basic in-app messaging or calling feature.
   - Facilitates quick communication between passengers and drivers for clarifications or updates.

### 7. **Ride Summary and History**
   - Provides passengers and drivers with a ride summary after completion.
   - Includes details like route, time, and seat information.

### 8. **Driver Availability Management**
   - Enables drivers to mark themselves as available or unavailable.
   - Helps manage schedules and optimize the system’s efficiency.

### 9. **Real-Time Passenger Tracking**
   - Displays the vehicle's real-time location during the ride.
   - Shows status updates (e.g., on-time, delayed) and estimated arrival times.

---

## Development Sequence
To ensure efficient implementation, the following development sequence is recommended:
1. Authentication for Passengers and Drivers.
2. Real-Time Seat and Location Tracking.
3. Dynamic Booking System.
4. Notifications for Passengers and Drivers.
5. Sumo Stand Queue Management.
6. In-App Communication System.
7. Ride Summary and History.
8. Driver Availability Management.
9. Real-Time Passenger Tracking.
